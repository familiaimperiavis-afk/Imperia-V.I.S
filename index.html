IMPERIA V.I.S. is a professional event staffing company providing reliable people and operational support for private, corporate and special events throughout Maryland, Washington, D.C., Virginia and New YORK CITY.

Our goal is simple: connect every event with dependable, professional and service-minded personnel who understand the importance of presentation, organization and guest experience.

From hospitality and event attendants to logistics, operations, crowd support and specialized staffing, our team is built to adapt to the needs of each client and each event.

Whether you are planning a private celebration, corporate event, convention, festival, conference or large-scale production, IMPERIA V.I.S. is ready to help you build the team your event needs.
